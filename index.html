Welcome to ETE HUB, the ultimate online resource for Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering students. Access comprehensive study materials including textbooks, lecture notes, previous year question papers, and examination resources for all eight semesters.

What is ETE HUB?

ETE HUB is a centralized platform providing free educational resources for Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) students. Whether you're in 1st year or 4th year, find all your study materials in one place.

Available Resources

Circuit Theory, Electrical Circuits, Digital Electronics, Analog Electronics, Microprocessors, Communication Systems, Signal Processing, Control Systems, Electronic Devices, Network Analysis, Electromagnetic Theory, and more.

  • 1st Year Even Semester - Analog Electronics, Digital Electronics, Electrical Machines
  • 2nd Year Odd Semester - Advanced Electronics, Communication, Networks
  • 2nd Year Even Semester - Microprocessors, Signal Processing, Control Systems
  • 3rd Year Odd Semester - Advanced Communication, VLSI Design, Embedded Systems
  • 3rd Year Even Semester - Wireless Communication, Optical Fiber, Microwave Engineering
  • 4th Year Odd Semester - Advanced Topics, Projects, Electives
  • 4th Year Even Semester - Final Year Projects, Industrial Training
